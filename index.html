Why Clean Energy Matters

Adopt Renewable Energy

Millions of households still spend a disproportionate share of their income on energy. Simple, accessible technology can cut costs while cutting emissions and students are often best placed to bring these ideas into their own communities.

  • Switch to LED lighting at home
  • Unplug idle chargers and devices
  • Ask your landlord or hall about efficiency upgrades

Community-Scale Renewable Power

Wind and solar co-operatives let neighbourhoods generate their own power, keeping costs down and control local. Understanding how these schemes work is a first step toward supporting or starting one.

  • Research community energy schemes near you
  • Share information with neighbours or classmates
  • Support local renewable energy petitions

Efficiency Is Everyone's Job

The cheapest unit of energy is the one never wasted. Small daily habits, multiplied across a household, a campus, or a city, create outsized impact on both bills and emissions.

  • Track your own energy use for a week
  • Advocate for efficient appliances where you study or work
  • Encourage widespread adoption for lasting change
